Schedule Me

A small self-hosted appointment booking tool

Schedule Me lets an organizer offer short appointments from their own calendar, and lets people book one from a public page without creating an account.

How it works

  • The organizer connects their Google Calendar and marks the times they are willing to meet as available.
  • The booking page shows those open times, worked out from the calendar itself — there is no separate list of slots to keep in sync.
  • Someone picks a time and enters their name and mobile number. No account, no password.
  • The appointment is written to the organizer's calendar, and the person booking gets a text confirming it.
  • A reminder goes out the day before. Either side can cancel, and the other is notified by text.

Signing in

To see or cancel an appointment, request a one-time code from the booking page. It is texted to the number on the booking. There are no passwords.

What it accesses

The application reads the organizer's connected Google Calendar to determine which times are free, and creates or removes events on it for bookings. It reads only whether time is busy or free — never the contents of other calendar entries. People booking appointments do not sign in with Google and none of their Google data is involved. This is set out in full in the Privacy Policy.

Book an appointment

If you were sent a booking link, open it to see the available times. The booking page is at this site's home page.
